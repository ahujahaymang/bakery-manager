# KitchenOS — Run Your Home Kitchen Like a Business

## You're great at baking. The paperwork is killing you.

You wake up at 5am to prep orders. You bake all day. You deliver in the evening. Then you sit down to figure out who paid, who owes you, what ingredients you need to restock, and whether you actually made money this week.

You're doing this in WhatsApp chats, paper notebooks, and mental math.

There's a better way.

---

## What is KitchenOS?

KitchenOS is a business assistant that lives in your Telegram. You talk to it like you'd talk to a helper — in plain language, in your own words — and it handles the rest.

No apps to learn. No forms to fill. No spreadsheets.

Just message it.

---

## See it in action

**Taking an order:**
> *"Priya wants 2 chocolate cakes at ₹500 each, deliver May 10"*

Done. Order created, customer saved, delivery date noted.

**Checking what you're owed:**
> *"Show unpaid orders"*

Instant list of every customer with an outstanding balance.

**Recording a payment:**
> *"Priya paid ₹1000 cash"*

Payment recorded against her order.

**Knowing your profit:**
> *"How much did I make this week?"*

Revenue, ingredient costs, packaging costs, gross profit — calculated automatically.

**Running out of flour:**
> *"Add 10kg flour at ₹45/kg"*

Inventory updated.

**Costing a new recipe:**
> *"What does one brownie cost me to make?"*

Exact cost per unit based on your ingredient prices.

---

## The problems it solves

### "I don't know who has paid and who hasn't"

Every order is tracked. Every payment is recorded. One message tells you exactly who owes you money and how much.

### "I forget to restock ingredients until I run out"

Your inventory is always current. Check stock levels anytime. Know exactly what you have before you take on a new order.

### "I have no idea if I'm actually making money"

KitchenOS knows your ingredient costs, your selling prices, and your order volume. Weekly profit reports show you the real numbers — not guesses.

### "Costing a new recipe takes forever"

Add your ingredients once. KitchenOS calculates the cost per unit automatically every time you update prices.

### "I spend hours at year-end trying to figure out my income"

Every transaction is recorded as it happens. Your financial history is always there, ready to export.

## What you can do with it

### Orders
- Create orders with multiple items, quantities, and prices
- Track delivery dates
- Mark orders as delivered
- Cancel orders when customers change their mind
- See all upcoming orders at a glance
- Filter by paid, unpaid, delivered, or pending

### Customers
- Add customers with name and phone number
- Find any customer instantly by name or phone

### Inventory
- Track ingredients and packaging separately
- Update quantities and costs
- Check stock levels anytime

### Recipes
- Build recipes with exact ingredient quantities
- Calculate cost per unit automatically
- Update costs when ingredient prices change
- Photograph a handwritten recipe — KitchenOS reads it and creates it for you

### Payments
- Record cash, Paytm, or bank transfer payments
- See full payment history
- Know exactly who still owes you

### Invoices
- Generate a professional PDF invoice for any order
- Just say *"send invoice to Priya"* — the bot creates and sends it instantly
- Includes your business name, customer details, itemised list, and amount due
- Currency matches your country (₹ for India, $ for US, £ for UK, etc.)

### Instagram Order Detection
- Connect your Instagram account once — the bot monitors your DMs automatically
- When a customer confirms an order in your DMs, the bot detects it
- You get a Telegram notification: *"New order from @customer — 2 brownies, Sunday. Confirm?"*
- Reply yes → order created. Reply no → discarded.
- No more manually copying orders from Instagram to your notebook

### Reports
- Weekly profit breakdown
- Revenue vs. costs at a glance

### Image scanning
- Photograph a handwritten recipe → it's created automatically
- Photograph a payment receipt → payment is recorded
- Screenshot a WhatsApp order → order is created
