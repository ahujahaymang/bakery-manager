# Privacy Policy

**KitchenOS** ("we", "our", "the service")  
Last updated: May 2026

---

## What we collect

When you use KitchenOS, we store the following information on your behalf:

- **Business information**: your business name
- **Customer data**: names and phone numbers of your customers
- **Order data**: orders, delivery dates, items, quantities, and prices
- **Inventory data**: ingredients, packaging materials, quantities, and costs
- **Recipe data**: recipes and their components
- **Payment records**: payment amounts, methods, and dates

We do not collect your personal identity documents, financial account details, or any information beyond what you explicitly provide through the bot.

---

## How your data is stored

Your data is stored in a private, encrypted database on a secure server. Each business account has its own isolated database — your data is never mixed with or accessible to other businesses using the service.

All data is stored on servers located in AWS data centres. Data volumes are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.

---

## Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate KitchenOS:

### Telegram
Messages you send to the bot are delivered via Telegram's platform. Telegram encrypts messages in transit and has its own [Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy). Telegram does not permanently store bot messages after delivery.

### OpenAI
Your messages are processed by OpenAI's API to understand your requests. OpenAI does not use API inputs to train their models. Message data may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for safety monitoring, after which it is deleted. See [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy).

### Amazon Web Services (AWS)
Your business data is stored on AWS infrastructure. AWS is SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified. See [AWS Privacy](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/).

---

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data to any third party
- We do not share your customer information with advertisers
- We do not use your business data for any purpose other than operating the service
- We do not send marketing messages to your customers

---

## Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Automated backups are kept for 24 hours and then deleted.

---

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — ask us and we will provide a copy
- **Correct** your data — update any information through the bot
- **Delete** your data — send a deletion request and we will remove your account and all associated data within 7 days

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the bot or at the contact details below.

---

## Data deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us through the bot or at the contact details below. We will process deletion requests within 7 days.

Deletion permanently removes your business name, all customers, orders, recipes, inventory, and payment records. This action cannot be undone.

---

## Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data:

- All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256)
- All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Access to servers is restricted to authorised personnel only
- No public ports are open on our servers
- Secrets (API keys, tokens) are stored in AWS Secrets Manager, not in code

---

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you through the bot if we make material changes.

---

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, contact us through the bot.

---

*This policy applies to all users of KitchenOS regardless of location.*
